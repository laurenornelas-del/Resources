PART 01

What Alliance is

A new name, a real charter, and a shift in what we are actually trying to build.

First, the name
Partners
⟶
Alliance

The rename is not cosmetic. Alliance is not a channel that resells Superhuman. It is a network that builds alongside it.

Superhuman Alliance · Ecosystem Strategy (Vision) · titles are moving from Partner to Alliance too
The charter

Build and run the Superhuman Alliance: the partner ecosystem that builds on, sells, and delivers our platform.

01
Expand platform value
An ecosystem of ISVs, SIs, developers and consultants building market relevant solutions on the platform, across regions and verticals.
02
Take joint solutions to market
Opinionated solution packages built with partners, then joint go to market behind them to drive partner influenced pipeline.
03
Solution, sell, deliver
Partners as a scalable engine: pipeline they generate, customers they manage, seats they implement and keep in use.
Why it matters

The shift is from selling through partners to building with them.

The old shape
−A largely transactional partner base, measured on resale
−A flat program: everyone gets the same thing
−The ecosystem treated as a distribution channel
Where we are going
+A co-building ecosystem of SIs, ISVs and technology partners
+Earned differentiation that rewards mutual investment, no pay to play tiers
+The ecosystem treated as part of the product

The channel opportunity gets captured through co-creation, not resale.
